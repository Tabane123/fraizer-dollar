About FraizaDollar

Crafting premium custom apparel that helps brands stand out with confidence.

Our Story

Founded with a passion for textiles and identity, FraizaDollar began as a small workshop dedicated to turning everyday fabric into meaningful brand experiences.

What started with simple custom prints has grown into a full-service branding studio. We combine traditional craftsmanship with modern design techniques to deliver clothing that speaks for your brand — clearly and confidently.

Today we partner with startups, established businesses, and creative agencies who understand that clothing is more than fabric — it’s wearable storytelling.

Our Mission

To empower brands with high-quality, thoughtfully designed apparel that carries their identity with clarity, durability, and style. Every stitch, print, and finish is intentional.

What We Stand For

Precision

Clean lines, accurate colours, and consistent quality. We obsess over the details so your brand never has to.

Craft

We respect the material. From fabric selection to final finishing, every step is handled with care and expertise.

Identity

Your brand is unique. We create clothing solutions that feel authentic to who you are — not generic templates.

Partnership

We work with you, not just for you. Clear communication and collaborative design sit at the heart of every project.
